# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a place where people learn in public. Most contributors are
writing their first production code and opening their first pull request. We
commit to making that a good experience for everyone, regardless of background,
experience level, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability,
appearance, body size, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## What we expect

- **Assume good faith.** A confusing PR usually means the author is learning,
  not that they were careless.
- **Review the code, not the person.** "This query runs once per student — can
  we batch it?" not "why would you do it this way?"
- **Ask questions before asserting.** You may be missing context the author has.
- **Say when you are stuck.** Nobody here is expected to already know this.
- **Credit people.** If someone's idea made your PR better, say so.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Dismissing or belittling someone for what they do not yet know
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project
maintainers privately. Reports are handled confidentially. If a maintainer is
involved, report to any other maintainer.

Maintainers will respond within a few days and may warn, temporarily block, or
permanently remove anyone who violates this code.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, in Discussions, and
in any project chat or meeting.
